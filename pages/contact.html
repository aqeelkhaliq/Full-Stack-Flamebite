FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FlameBite.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and Saturday to Sunday from 09:00 AM to 12:00 AM.

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes! We offer fast and reliable home delivery across Abbottabad. You can order directly through our website or by calling us.

Can I customize my order?

Absolutely! You can customize your burgers, pizzas, and sandwiches by adding or removing ingredients according to your taste.

Do you cater for events?

Yes, we provide catering services for parties, corporate events, and private gatherings. Contact us for a custom quote.

Is there a loyalty program?

Yes, we offer a loyalty program where you earn points on every order. Redeem them for discounts and free meals!

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery, credit/debit cards, and all major mobile payment systems (JazzCash, EasyPaisa).
