At FlameBite, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

By using FlameBite, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

01

Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and payment details when you place an order or create an account.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent, and referring URLs.
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, operating system, and device type.
  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device to enhance your browsing experience (see Section 3).
02

How We Use Your Information

FlameBite uses your information for the following purposes:

  • Process and fulfill your orders, including delivery and payment.
  • Manage your account and provide customer support.
  • Send you order confirmations, updates, and promotional offers (with your consent).
  • Improve our website, menu, and overall user experience.
  • Prevent fraud and ensure the security of our platform.
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements.
03

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and login status.
  • Analyze website traffic and user behavior.
  • Show personalized content and advertisements.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect certain features of our website.

04

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • Encryption: All sensitive data is encrypted using SSL/TLS technology.
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel have access to your data.
  • Regular Audits: We conduct security audits to identify and address vulnerabilities.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

05

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share your data with trusted partners for:

  • Payment processing (e.g., credit card processors).
  • Delivery services to fulfill your orders.
  • Analytics and marketing (with your consent).

All third-party partners are contractually obligated to protect your information and comply with applicable privacy laws.

06

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: Request the deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@flamebite.com.

07

Children's Privacy

FlameBite is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.
